Study in the UK after 12th: UCAS, costs and the new Graduate route

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in India.

The UK has short degrees, a single application system and growing interest from Indian students. It has also raised the money you must show and is shortening its post-study work visa. This guide explains how to apply after Class 12 and what to check before you do.

What is changing

  • Demand is rising. Market analysts have noted that Indian demand for the UK has picked up and that the UK may overtake Canada and the USA as the top destination for Indian students.
  • Post-study work is shorter. The Graduate route falls from two years to 18 months for people who apply on or after 1 January 2027. PhD graduates keep three years.
  • More money to show. Maintenance funds rose from 11 November 2025. For 2026 to 2027, one guide reports a further rise, so check the current figure on GOV.UK.
Espiron tip

A student who starts a three-year degree in September 2027 will finish in 2030. Under today's rules they would get 18 months on the Graduate route, but rules can change again.

How to apply after 12th

  1. Choose course and universities. You can apply to several courses on one UCAS application.
  2. Check Class 12 entry rules. Each university lists what it accepts from CBSE, ISC and state boards. Entry marks vary a lot by course and university.
  3. Consider a foundation route if your marks are below direct entry. A guide for Indian students says students scoring around 60 to 70% in Class 12 are often offered a foundation year or an International Year One.
  4. Take an accepted English test if the university asks for one.
  5. Accept an offer, receive your CAS, then apply for the visa.

Money and work

  • From 11 November 2025 the maintenance funds you must show rose to £1,529 a month for London, for up to nine months. For 2026 to 2027 a guide reports £1,570 in London and £1,203 elsewhere. Confirm on GOV.UK
  • Add tuition, accommodation and travel. Part-time work is limited, so do not plan to fund tuition from it
  • Many universities also ask for a deposit when you accept an offer. Read the refund terms before you pay

Who it suits

The UK suits students who know what they want to study, want a shorter degree, and can fund the full cost. It is less forgiving for students who are unsure about their course, because switching is harder than in the US.

Common questions

Can I apply to the UK after Class 12?

Yes, through UCAS. Entry rules depend on the course and university, so check each one's page for Indian boards.

What if my Class 12 marks are not high?

A foundation year or International Year One may be an option. These add time and cost, so compare them with other routes.

Can I work in the UK after my degree?

Under the Graduate route you can stay and work for a limited time after a bachelor's or master's degree. From 1 January 2027 it is 18 months.

Can I bring family to the UK?

Most students on bachelor's and master's courses cannot bring dependants. Check the current rule on GOV.UK.

How much money must I show?

The amount depends on whether you study in London and for how long. It rose in November 2025 and is reported to rise again for 2026 to 2027. Check GOV.UK for the figure that applies to you.
