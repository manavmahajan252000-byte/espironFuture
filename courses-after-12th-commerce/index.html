Courses after 12th Commerce: careers, exams and how to choose

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in Amritsar and Punjab.

Commerce students have more choices than just B.Com. This guide covers chartered accountancy, management, finance, economics and law, what each needs after Class 12, and how to pick the one that fits your marks and your interests.

Chartered Accountancy after 12th

CA is a professional qualification with three levels: Foundation, Intermediate and Final, along with practical training. It is a long route, and many students take a degree such as B.Com alongside it.

  • You can register provisionally after Class 10, but you must clear Class 12 before you can sit the Foundation exam.
  • Students from any stream can join after Class 12.
  • Graduates can also enter at the Intermediate level through the direct entry route. The recent rules ask for at least 55% in a commerce degree and 60% in other degrees. Confirm this with ICAI.
Espiron tip

CA rewards steady daily work more than last-minute effort. Ask yourself whether you can study consistently for several years before you commit.

Management and BBA

BBA and BMS are three-year degrees that lead to jobs or to an MBA. Some institutes also offer five-year integrated programmes that combine a bachelor's degree and an MBA, usually through tests such as IPMAT and NPAT.

Look beyond the course name. Compare the syllabus, internships, the faculty, and how recent graduates are doing.

B.Com, economics and finance degrees

B.Com remains the most popular commerce degree. Most colleges let you specialise in areas such as accounting and finance, taxation, banking and insurance, or e-commerce.

BA and BSc Economics suit students who like data and reasoning. The stronger programmes expect good Maths.

Central universities rely on CUET UG for undergraduate admission, so a good CUET plan matters if you aim for them.

Espiron tip

Whatever degree you choose, add practical skills early: Excel, accounting software, GST and income tax basics, and simple data analysis. They help you in every commerce career.

Law after 12th

Five-year integrated law degrees, such as BA LLB and BBA LLB, are open to students from any stream. National law universities admit through CLAT, and other universities use their own tests. Punjab has Rajiv Gandhi National University of Law in Patiala. Check the marks and age rules in the current notice before you apply.

Commerce with or without Maths

Commerce with Maths keeps more doors open: economics, BCA, data and analytics, and stronger finance programmes. Without Maths you can still do CA, BBA, B.Com, law and many other courses.

If you are in Class 11 and deciding whether to drop Maths, talk to a counsellor first. The choice is easier to make before the year starts than after.

How to choose

  • Which subjects do you actually enjoy, not only score well in?
  • Do you prefer a long professional route (CA) or a degree followed by work or an MBA?
  • What can your family afford over the whole course?
  • Which college offers real internships and honest placement records?

Ask every college for the total fee for the full course, its approvals and its placement details in writing.

Studying in Punjab after Commerce

  • Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar offers B.Com, BBA, BA and other undergraduate programmes through its entrance process. Check the current prospectus on gnduadmissions.org.
  • Central University of Punjab, Bathinda is the state's central university. Central universities rely on CUET UG.
  • IIM Amritsar is a postgraduate management institute that admits through CAT.
  • RGNUL, Patiala is a national law university.

Punjab's industries, such as hosiery and textiles in Ludhiana, sports goods in Jalandhar and agro-processing, need accountants, tax and compliance staff, and export and supply-chain managers. Local knowledge can be an advantage. Read more in our guide to career options in Punjab.

Common questions

Can a Commerce student do engineering?

B.Tech through JEE Main needs Physics and Maths in Class 12, so students without them are not eligible. There are other technology routes, such as BCA, that may ask for Maths. Check each college.

Can a Commerce student write NEET?

No. NEET needs Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Biotechnology in Class 12.

Can I do CA and B.Com together?

Many students do. Check how the study schedule and the attendance rules of your college fit with your CA preparation.

Is CA better than an MBA?

They are different. CA is a professional qualification you can start after Class 12. An MBA is a postgraduate degree you take after graduation. Some people do both at different stages.
