Courses after 12th Science: what to choose and which exam opens it

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in Amritsar and Punjab.

Science opens the widest set of doors after Class 12, but each door has its own exam and its own subject rules. This guide shows the main routes for PCM and PCB students, what each one needs, and how to choose between them.

If you studied PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)

Most PCM students think only of engineering. It is the largest route, but it is not the only one.

Engineering

JEE Main leads to B.E. and B.Tech seats in NITs, IIITs and many other colleges through counselling. For the IITs you must also qualify in JEE Advanced, which is open to students who rank among the top candidates in JEE Main. The exact cut-off changes every year.

You can sit JEE Main without a minimum Class 12 percentage. Seat rules for NITs, IIITs and similar institutes do set one. In the latest rules we found, it is 75% for General, OBC and EWS students and 65% for SC and ST students, or a place in the top 20 percentile of your board.

Architecture

B.Arch needs Maths in Class 12 and a drawing and aptitude test. Colleges accept NATA or JEE Main Paper 2A, depending on the institute.

Other PCM options

  • B.Sc in Physics, Maths, Computer Science or Data Science
  • BCA and other computer degrees
  • Defence entry through the NDA exam run by UPSC. The Air Force and Navy wings ask for Physics and Maths, so check the current notice
  • Merchant navy, aviation and other technical courses, each with its own eligibility and medical rules
Espiron tip

Do not choose a branch only by its name. A strong college in a less fashionable branch often serves you better than a weak college in a fashionable one.

If you studied PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)

NEET UG is the single entrance exam for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and BSc Nursing seats.

Who can sit NEET UG

  • Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English
  • At least 50% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology together for the General category, and 40% for reserved categories, under the published rules
  • At least 17 years old by 31 December of the exam year, with no upper age limit and no limit on attempts in the latest notices
  • Maths is not required. PCM students who studied Biology or Biotechnology as an additional subject can also be eligible

Beyond MBBS

There are many strong careers in healthcare and life sciences besides MBBS: BDS, BAMS and BHMS, BSc Nursing, physiotherapy, medical lab technology, pharmacy, biotechnology, microbiology and food technology. Some of these need NEET and some use other tests, so check each course.

Espiron tip

NEET is very competitive. Build a second plan early, such as a good allied health or life science degree, and agree with your family in advance whether a repeat year is an option.

Other science routes

CUET UG

Central universities, and a growing number of other universities, admit B.Sc and other undergraduate students through CUET UG. The test has language papers, domain subject papers based on the Class 12 syllabus, and a general test. Students choose a limited number of papers, up to five in the 2026 cycle. Pick the papers your target courses ask for.

University and state tests

Many universities, including Guru Nanak Dev University in Amritsar, run their own entrance process for some courses. Read the current prospectus before you plan your preparation.

How to choose between routes

Start with four honest questions.

  • What do you enjoy doing for hours, even when no one is watching?
  • What do your marks and mock tests say today, without wishful thinking?
  • What can your family afford for the whole course, not just the first year?
  • Where do you want to live and work in five years?

Then build a three-tier list: one stretch option, one realistic option and one safe option. Check the exam dates for all three, so you do not miss a registration window.

Studying in Punjab after Science

  • IIT Ropar admits B.Tech students through JEE Advanced.
  • NIT Jalandhar and SLIET Sangrur admit through JEE Main and JoSAA counselling.
  • AIIMS Bathinda admits through NEET UG.
  • Punjab MBBS and BDS state quota seats are filled through counselling by Baba Farid University of Health Sciences, Faridkot. State quota seats are for Punjab domicile candidates. The 15% All India Quota is handled by MCC counselling.
  • Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar offers B.Tech through JEE Main and B.Arch through NATA, with other courses through its entrance process.
  • IISER Mohali admits science students through IAT.

Common questions

Can a PCM student write NEET?

Only if you studied Biology or Biotechnology as an additional subject in Class 12 and meet the other rules. Confirm this in the NEET notice for your year.

Can a PCB student do engineering?

B.Tech through JEE Main asks for Physics, Maths and one of Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology or a technical vocational subject. So Maths is needed. PCB students without Maths are not eligible for it.

Is there a minimum percentage for JEE Main?

There is no minimum to appear. Seat rules for NITs, IIITs and similar institutes set one, so check the latest rules for your year.

Which is better, JEE or NEET?

Neither. Choose by interest, marks and what you can sustain for years. Popularity is a poor guide.

Can I move to Commerce or Arts-based careers after Science?

Yes. Chartered accountancy is open to students from any stream, and BBA, law, design and humanities degrees accept science students too.
