Study in Australia after 12th: routes, costs and visa rules

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in India.

Australia offers more than one way in after Class 12: a direct bachelor's degree, a diploma that credits into a degree, a foundation year, or a vocational course. It has also managed student numbers, raised visa costs and added a Genuine Student check. This guide explains what to look at.

What is changing

  • Managed growth. The government kept the 2027 planning level at 295,000, 8% below the post-pandemic peak, and says commencements are on track to stay below it.
  • Fewer new students. The Education Minister's office reports 2026 commencements down 8% on the same period in 2025.
  • Higher costs. One 2026 guide reports a student visa fee of A$2,500 from 1 July 2026 and a financial capacity requirement of about A$29,710 a year. Confirm both on the Home Affairs website.
  • Genuine Student requirement. Applicants must show a genuine intention to study, which replaced the earlier Genuine Temporary Entrant test.

Visa and money

  1. Receive an offer and a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE).
  2. Show financial capacity, English ability, health and character documents.
  3. Meet the Genuine Student requirement with clear, honest answers about your study plan and why Australia.
  4. Apply online and keep every document consistent. Unexplained gaps or mismatches are a common reason for further checks.
Espiron tip

Do not pay a large fee or book flights before the visa is granted. Keep proof of every payment.

Who it suits

Australia suits students who can fund the full cost, prefer a flexible route into a degree, and can explain their plan clearly. If your budget only covers the first year, think carefully before you apply.

Common questions

Can I go to Australia directly after 12th?

Yes. You can apply for a bachelor's degree, a diploma, a foundation course or a vocational course. Marks and English needs vary by provider.

What is the Genuine Student requirement?

A check that you really intend to study and your plan makes sense. It replaced the earlier Genuine Temporary Entrant test.

Is there a cap on international students?

The government says the National Planning Level is not a cap, but a way to prioritise visa applications. It is 295,000 for 2026 and 2027.

How much money must I show?

One 2026 guide reports about A$29,710 a year, plus tuition and travel. Confirm on the Home Affairs website.

Is a diploma-to-degree route safe?

It can work, but you must confirm in writing that the diploma credits into the degree you want.
