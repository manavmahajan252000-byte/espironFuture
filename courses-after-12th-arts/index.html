Courses after 12th Arts: careers, exams and how to choose

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in Amritsar and Punjab.

Arts and Humanities students have more options than most people think. Law, design, journalism, teaching, hospitality and civil services all open to you after Class 12. This guide shows what each route needs and how to choose.

Law after 12th

Five-year integrated law degrees such as BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB are open to students from any stream. CLAT is the main entrance for national law universities. Other law universities use AILET, LSAT India or their own tests.

  • Minimum marks in Class 12: 45% for General, OBC and similar categories, and 40% for SC and ST, under the published CLAT rules
  • No upper age limit, and students appearing in Class 12 can apply
  • CLAT UG is only for five-year programmes, not the three-year LLB
Espiron tip

Law is a long road, and the first years are heavy on reading and writing. Try reading a few court judgments or legal news stories first and see whether you enjoy it.

Design after 12th

Design is an area where Arts students are not at a disadvantage. B.Des and design programmes accept students from any stream. Entrance routes include NID DAT, NIFT's entrance test and UCEED, and many private institutes use their own tests.

Fields include communication design, fashion, interior, animation, UX and product design. Start a simple portfolio of sketches and projects early. It helps you in tests and interviews.

Media and journalism

BJMC and mass communication degrees lead to journalism, content, public relations, advertising and digital media. IIMC runs its own entrance test, and many universities admit through CUET UG, their own tests or merit.

Employers look at your work. Write, record or edit from the first year, and keep samples.

BA degrees and what they lead to

A BA is not a single path. Your subject choices shape where you can go.

  • Economics: leads to finance, policy, data and analytics. Good Maths helps
  • Psychology: leads to counselling, HR and research, usually with a master's degree
  • Political Science, History, Sociology: a base for civil services, research, law and public policy
  • English and languages: content, translation, teaching and media
  • Social work and public policy: NGOs, development and government programmes

Many central and state universities admit to BA programmes through CUET UG, so choose your CUET subjects to match the courses you want.

Teaching after 12th

The Integrated Teacher Education Programme (ITEP) is a four-year programme that gives you a degree and a B.Ed together, such as BA B.Ed. Admission is through NCET, conducted by NTA. Under the National Education Policy, a four-year integrated degree is meant to become the minimum qualification for teaching by 2030.

For the 2026 to 2027 session, regulators directed recognised ITEP institutions to admit students only through NCET.

Hospitality, management and public services

  • Hotel management: programmes in hotels, travel and tourism. NCHM JEE is one of the main tests. Tourism is a strong sector for Amritsar
  • Integrated management: some institutes offer five-year BBA plus MBA programmes through tests such as IPMAT and JIPMAT
  • Civil services and government jobs: UPSC, SSC, banking and state PSC exams are taken after graduation. Many Arts students choose subjects like History, Political Science and Economics with this in mind
  • Chartered accountancy: open to students from any stream. See our guide to courses after 12th Commerce

How to choose

  • What do you enjoy reading, making or arguing about, outside of exams?
  • Are you comfortable with a long professional path, such as law, or do you want a three-year degree first?
  • Which skills can you start building now: writing, design, speaking, languages, data?
  • What can your family afford for the full course, including travel and hostel?

A good plan has one main choice, one backup, and one skill you build from today.

Studying in Punjab after Arts

  • Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar offers BA and other programmes, fashion design, and an integrated B.A. B.Ed. programme. Check its current prospectus.
  • Central University of Punjab, Bathinda is the state's central university. Central universities rely on CUET UG.
  • Punjabi University, Patiala and Rajiv Gandhi National University of Law, Patiala are other options for arts, humanities and law.
  • Khalsa College, Amritsar and other colleges in Amritsar offer BA degrees.

Common questions

Can Arts students do engineering or medicine?

Not directly. B.Tech through JEE Main needs Physics and Maths, and NEET needs Physics, Chemistry and Biology. There are many strong options in law, design, media, teaching, hospitality and management instead.

Can Arts students do CA?

Yes. Chartered accountancy is open to students from any stream after Class 12.

Is a BA degree worth it?

It can be, if you choose the subjects and skills with a plan. A BA with strong skills in writing, data or languages opens many doors.

Do I need coaching for CLAT?

Many students prepare with coaching and others with self-study. What matters is regular reading, practice tests and honest review. A counsellor can help you plan.

Which Arts courses can lead to government jobs?

Most government exams, such as UPSC, SSC and banking, are open to graduates of any stream. Many Arts students prepare alongside a degree in subjects like History, Political Science or Economics.
