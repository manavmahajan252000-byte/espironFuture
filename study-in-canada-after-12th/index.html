Study in Canada after 12th: permits, costs and what has changed

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in India.

Canada is still the top host country for Indian students in government data, but it has capped study permits and tightened the rules for work after study. If you plan to apply after Class 12, the permit process and the choice of institution matter more than before.

What is changing

  • Fewer students are being admitted. IRCC figures reported by an immigration news site show study permit holders fell from 928,430 at the end of 2024 to 691,215 at the end of 2025.
  • Graduate students are favoured. From 1 January 2026, master's and PhD students at public institutions are exempt from the cap and from the PAL or TAL requirement. Undergraduates after Class 12 are not.
  • Where you study affects work rights. Graduates of private colleges that license public college curricula are not eligible for the PGWP, according to a 2026 summary.

Permit steps

  1. Receive an offer from a Designated Learning Institution.
  2. Get a PAL or TAL where required. Your institution usually helps with this.
  3. Show proof of funds and explain how you will pay for later years.
  4. Apply for the study permit through IRCC and prepare for questions about your plan.
  5. Plan for processing time and book flights only after approval.
Espiron tip

Do not pay full fees, or book flights, until your permit is approved. Ask every institution for its refund terms in writing.

Costs and funds

Add tuition, living costs, health cover and travel, then multiply by the length of the course. A four-year degree at the average international fee costs well over CA$150,000 in tuition alone, so a clear funding plan matters. Fees can differ a lot by university and province, and some universities cap yearly increases for new international students.

Work and PGWP

  • Study permit holders may work limited hours off campus under the permit conditions. Confirm the current limit on the IRCC website
  • The Post-Graduation Work Permit depends on your programme's field of study and the type of institution
  • Do not assume a path to permanent residence. It depends on separate immigration rules that change often

Who it suits

Canada suits students who can fund the full course, choose a public institution with a PGWP-eligible programme, and accept that permit rules may change again. It is a poor choice if the plan depends on staying after study.

Common questions

Can I study in Canada after 12th?

Yes. You can apply to college diplomas or university degrees. Check the institution's entry rules for your Indian board.

What is a PAL or TAL?

A letter from a province or territory that most new post-secondary students need before applying for a study permit. Master's and PhD students at public institutions are exempt from 1 January 2026.

Is a private college a good idea?

Be careful. Graduates of some private colleges that license public curricula are not PGWP-eligible. Always check the programme and institution against the PGWP rules.

How much money must I show?

CA$22,895 for one person plus first-year tuition from September 2025. It rose again on 1 September 2026, so check the current amount on IRCC.

Does a Canadian degree guarantee PR?

No. Permanent residence is a separate process with its own rules, and those rules change.
