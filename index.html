Your first year after 12th

Five stages. Pick the one you are in.

Tap a stage to see what happens, what to decide and where students go wrong.

Results and direction

Board results are out. Your first job is to narrow a wide field into two or three course families that fit your marks and your interests.

  • List the courses open to your stream and your marks
  • Match them with what you enjoy and what is growing
  • Talk it through with your family before you register for anything
The big decisionWhich two or three course families deserve my effort?
Common mistakePicking a course because friends or relatives did.
Plan this stage with a counsellor

Entrance exams

Most good courses open through an exam. Registration windows are short, and some close before results are out.

  • Find the exams your target courses accept: JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET UG, CLAT, NID DAT and others
  • Note registration, admit card and exam dates in one calendar
  • Keep a backup exam for a second route
The big decisionWhich exams do I write, and which can I skip?
Common mistakeMissing a registration date and losing a whole year.
Plan this stage with a counsellor

Counselling and applications

Seats are allocated in rounds. The order in which you list courses and colleges changes the seat you get.

  • Gather documents early: marksheets, category and domicile papers, photos
  • Learn how each counselling round works before it opens
  • Rank your choices by course first, then college
The big decisionIn what order should I fill my choices?
Common mistakeFilling choices in a hurry, or missing a document deadline.
Plan this stage with a counsellor

College and fees

A seat is not the same as a good fit. Compare colleges on facts before you pay.

  • Check approvals and recognition of the college and course
  • Compare total fees for the full course, not just the first year
  • Ask for real placement and alumni details, not brochure claims
The big decisionIs this college worth its total cost to my family?
Common mistakePaying a large fee before checking the college's approvals.
Plan this stage with a counsellor

Your first year

The first year sets the habits that decide your results and your skills for the next three or four years.

  • Pick one add-on skill to build alongside your degree
  • Look for internships and projects from the first year
  • Meet seniors and faculty in your field early
The big decisionWhich skills will make me stand out in my course?
Common mistakeWaiting until the final year to build skills and contacts.
Plan this stage with a counsellor

Months are typical. Exam and counselling dates change every year, so your counsellor confirms the current calendar for your courses.

Questions

Before you book

Is the first counselling call really free?

Yes. Book the call, share your details and a counsellor will talk you through your options.

I have not chosen a stream yet. Can I still book?

Yes. Pick "Not decided yet" on the form. Many students start there.

Do you guarantee admission to a college?

No. Admissions depend on each institution's rules, your marks and exam results. We help you choose well and apply on time.

Can my parents join the call?

Yes, and we encourage it. Decisions about courses and fees go better when the family is in the same conversation.

To contact you about career counselling and to prepare for your counselling call.
