Study in Germany after 12th: the route most Indian students miss

Last updated 21 November 2026. Written for students and parents in India.

Germany is attractive because most public universities charge no tuition. But an Indian Class 12 certificate alone does not give direct entry to a German bachelor's degree. Most students need a one-year foundation course first. This guide explains the real route, the money and the paperwork.

What is changing

  • More Indian interest. An upGrad report found Germany's share among Indian study-abroad aspirants rising from 13.2% in 2022 to 32.6% in 2024 to 2025.
  • The 70% rule. Several guides report a new minimum of 70% in Class 12 from the winter semester 2026 to 2027. Check with DAAD and the university before you plan.
  • Public universities remain low cost, but competitive entry and the extra year of foundation study add time and living costs.

APS, language and visa

  1. APS certificate. A document verification by the Academic Evaluation Centre, mandatory for Indian students. Processing is reported at 4 to 8 weeks.
  2. Language. German for Studienkolleg and many degrees. English-taught programmes exist, usually with an English test.
  3. Admission. Apply through the university or a central service. Deadlines can be early. A guide reports 31 May for some winter-semester applications.
  4. Blocked account. Deposit the required amount and get the confirmation.
  5. Student visa. Apply with admission, APS and blocked account documents. Indian citizens always need a visa to enter Germany.
Espiron tip

Be careful with agents who promise direct entry after Class 12. DAAD India says it is not available for most students.

What it costs

  • Tuition is zero at most public universities, but the semester contribution, health insurance, APS fee, translation and blocked account service charges still add up
  • The blocked account amount is money you spend on living costs, released monthly, and not a fee
  • Studienkolleg and German classes add a year and extra cost before the degree starts
  • After a degree, a job seeker visa of 18 months is reported for graduates

Who it suits

Germany suits students with strong Class 12 marks who are willing to learn German, accept a four-year plan and want low tuition. It does not suit students who want to start a degree within months.

Common questions

Can I go straight to a German bachelor's after 12th?

Usually not. DAAD India says a Class 12 certificate alone does not give direct admission, with the exception of a valid IIT-JEE Advanced result.

What is Studienkolleg?

A one-year foundation course that prepares you for the entrance exam to a German degree. It usually needs German at about B1 level and an APS certificate.

Is a German degree really free?

Tuition is free at most public universities, but you pay a semester contribution and your living costs, and you must show the blocked account amount.

What is the APS certificate?

A verification of your Indian documents, mandatory for Indian students applying for a German student visa.

Can I work after studying?

Graduates can apply for an 18-month job seeker visa, as reported by several guides. Confirm the current rule with the German authorities.
